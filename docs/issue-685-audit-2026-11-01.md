# Issue #685 audit (2026-11-01)

Issue: "Saved fight set-ups: one format for #570's measurement scenarios, a
combat test bench, and scripted fights for GMs". Filed against 0.31.2;
audited against main at v0.31.4 (df26af785). Branch `audit-685` carries the
fixes for the bugs found on the way (section 3).

## 1. Verdict

The request is real and worth building. Its core claims hold on 0.31.4:
nothing about a test fight can be kept between runs, the server's own dice
cannot be set, and no connected-agent tool reaches workshops or test fights.
Of the six "smaller gaps", two were fixed in 0.31.3 (the off-turn reaction
card, a companion's turn at a person-run table), and part of a third (the
reaction answer ending the hero's turn) was still broken and is fixed here.
The rest are real gaps, listed with what each needs in section 4.

The audit also found related bugs outside the report. Six are fixed on this
branch (section 3), one of them a regression shipped in 0.31.4.

## 3. Bugs found and fixed on `audit-685`

Each fix has a test that fails on main and passes on the branch.

**F1. A reaction card counted as the hero declaring their turn.**
`declaredThisTurn` and `wordsThisTurn` (`src/lib/dm/player-word.ts`) counted
every player message since the GM last spoke, a reaction card included. The
hero on turn (or up next, while the enemies' pass runs) who answered a hit
with Shield or Uncanny Dodge therefore had "declared": `characterAwaitingPlayer`
stopped guarding their turn, the AI was free to spend it for them, and
`turnOverFor` let the narration hand the turn on unplayed. This is the cause
behind the report's "answering the reaction ended that hero's turn"; 0.31.3's
off-turn card fixed the route around it for heroes not on turn only. Fix:
messages whose intent is a reaction card are not the hero's word on their
own turn. Test: `test-off-turn-hand.mjs` "A reaction card from the hero whose
turn it is leaves that turn waiting on them".

**F2. At an AI table only the last hero hit in a reply could react
(regression in 0.31.4).** The reaction window from issue 713
(`src/lib/dm/last-hit.ts`, commit ac4018a7c) closes a hit at "the next attack
on anyone". The AI plays every enemy of a pass in one reply, so the second
enemy's attack closed the first hero's hit before any player read it. Its own
stated aim, "the player hit still has time to read it at an AI table after
the reply ends", held only for the last hero hit. Fix: each record carries
the GM turn that rolled it (`currentTurnId`), and attacks in the same GM turn
leave each other open; an attack in a later turn, or the next creature's
first action, still closes them. A person's console call is its own turn, so
the person-run behaviour is unchanged. Tests: `test-enforce-dying-turn-order.mjs`
(the AI-table case now asserts both hits stay open, plus a later-turn case).

**F3. "Play their turn for me" refused a dominated hero.** At a person-run
table the tracker offers the button for a hero dominated by the other side
(`DmInitiativePanel.tsx`, from `companionTurn`, which uses `dmPlaysTurn`),
but `playCompanionTurn` checked for a companion and answered "It is not a
companion's turn." The AI's backstop already plays dominated heroes through
`dmPlaysTurn`. Fix: the route accepts any turn the GM plays. Test:
`test-enforce-combat-sides-dominate.mjs`.

**F4. A co-GM's cards waited in the queue.** The actions route resolves a
card at once only for `isPrimaryDm`, while a co-GM holds the same in-game
powers (`isDmSeat`) and `gmCompanionTurn` hands them a companion's turn. A
co-GM playing that turn had every card queued for the GM to play again.
Fix: `isDmSeat`. Test: `test-off-turn-hand.mjs` co-GM case.

**F5. Spend at a deleted table vanished from the admin's usage.** Usage rows
keep their campaign id, but `usageOverview` lists only existing campaigns and
credits an account with its own rows plus the tables it leads. A deleted
table's rows reached no total. Every ended or replaced test fight is deleted,
so its AI spend disappeared routinely. Fix: rows of a table that no longer
exists count for the account that asked for the work. Test:
`test-shared-host.mjs`.

**F6. The picture rescue listed test tables as stranded campaigns.**
`campaignPictures` labelled every non-workshop row a campaign, so a test
table showed in the admin's picture check and could be "rescued" with its
maps turned on. Fix: test tables are skipped; their workshop is the one to
move. Test: `test-harness-pictures.mjs`.

Also: `test-mcp-list-campaigns.mjs` now asserts a test table is not listed
(its header promised it; nothing checked).

No client change is needed for any of these: the apps compile the server's
pages, and no route or field changed shape.

## 4. What fully addressing #685 takes

### 4.A The set-up (one format, three uses)

What a prepared fight already stores (`TemplateExtras`,
`src/lib/dm/encounter-template-logic.ts:326`): enemy placements, a party
entry point, hidden slots, name and max HP overrides, rewards text, and up to
eight phase lines (text only, for the GM's note). Missing for a set-up:

- every combatant's current HP, conditions (with their source and duration
  meta), position and initiative, party included;
- the party's resources (slots, features, hit dice, death saves);
- the test table's own settings (allies, rules variants);
- an ordered list of opening GM actions, in the console's own call shape
  (`invokeEngine` adjudications), replayed after the deploy;
- on test tables only, a dice script (4.C).

Shape: a `setup_json` column on `encounter_templates`, parsed with zod like
the extras, carried by every copy path (`content-copy.ts copyEncounterRow`,
workshop bundles and imports, the import into a real table). The opening
actions should be stored as console calls, not as a state snapshot where a
call can say it: a call replays through every rule the engine applies, and a
snapshot of a sheet would go stale when the pack or the sheet changes.

Saving: "Save as this fight's set-up" on the test banner (`TestFightBanner.tsx`)
reads the live table back into the format. Loading: the default when a set-up
exists, so no setting (AGENTS.md rule 1). A way to drop it lives beside the
fight's other fields in the editor.

### 4.B A reproducible start ("Keep this map", gap 5)

- Store the generator seed and size on the template on the first test
  (`applyStudioMap` already takes a seed), or the prepared map the author
  drew; then save every token's tile, party included, not only the entry.
- Seed the stand-ins' names from the workshop (or name them by class), so
  the same hero takes the same tile.
- Initiative from the set-up when it has one; rolled as now when not.

### 4.C The dice script (test tables only)

- `defaultRng` (`src/lib/dice.ts`) asks the running turn scope
  (`job-control.ts currentTurnId`, open in every AI turn, console call and
  parked roll) for its campaign, and takes faces from that campaign's script
  only when the campaign is a playtest. A live table can never be scripted,
  which keeps the "the dice are honest" promise by construction.
- A seed (mulberry32, as `scripts/lib/enforce-world.mjs seedDice` does), and
  a queue of forced faces aimed by roller and kind ("the next enemy attack's
  d20", "the next death save"). Aiming needs the roll sites to say who and
  what they roll: the enemy attack (`enemy-attack.ts`), death saves
  (`death.ts:259,334`), saves the server rolls, initiative. Today they pass
  nothing but the expression.
- The test scripts' process-wide `crypto.randomInt` swap cannot serve this:
  every table on the server would draw from the queue.

### 4.D Scripted events for live GMs

Phases today are text. Events ("reinforcements at round 3", "the bridge
falls when the boss does") need a trigger (round reached, a slot dropped, HP
below a mark) and an action list in the console's call shape. The triggers
fit the existing hooks: the pointer's round change (`advancePointer`) and an
enemy's death. At live tables these change state and never dice. This needs
its own small editor in the prepared fight's form: per AGENTS.md rule 6 it
needs the written justification and a mockup before it is built.

### 4.E Agent reach

New workbench tools (`src/lib/agents/workbench.ts`), on the campaigns scope:
list my workshops; list a workshop's prepared fights; start a test fight
(fight id, party stand-ins or characters, runner, use the set-up); end it.
The routes exist (`/api/workshops/[workshopId]/test-fight`); the tools wrap
them. `scripts/score-cards.mjs` (#570) can then load a saved set-up instead
of staging its own scenario.

### 4.F The remaining smaller gaps

- **Gap 2, the GM console at an AI-run test.** Open an AI-run test with the
  author in a GM seat while the AI still narrates and runs the enemies
  (the "steered" mode seats the owner: `createCampaign`). Check first: the
  monsters' Hand is shown to any GM seat (`Composer.tsx:196`), so the author
  could play enemy cards while the AI plays the same enemies.
- **Gap 3, allies.** Keep the workshop's own `companions` setting on the test
  table instead of forcing it off, and let the picker seat library companions
  as companions (copies, like the heroes). Today the API's refusal sends the
  author to "Build a companion, then From your library", which the test
  table refuses while allies are off.
- **Gap 4, spell cards at a person-run table.** Cards that resolve at once:
  attack, save and auto-hit spells at one enemy in a fight, heals and buffs
  on a target, Spiritual Weapon placed, instant blasts laid on the map,
  shared castings, features, basics, reactions, items. Cards that fall back
  to a sentence in the GM's "Waiting on you" list: summons and utility
  spells, lasting areas (Web, Spirit Guardians, Moonbeam:
  `direct-context.ts:28`), blasts with no square picked, area heals, attack
  and save spells out of a fight, riders (Divine Smite, manoeuvres). The
  stored card is dropped from that list (`DmConsolePanel.tsx:367`), so the GM
  re-enters it by hand. Two parts: extend `directCardCall` to the remaining
  spell groups (summons through `cast_buff`, utility through
  `use_spell_slot`, lasting areas through `aoe_damage` with their squares),
  which is #570's open "cards straight to the engine" work; and offer
  "Resolve as played" on a waiting row that carries a card, which helps every
  person-run table, not only tests.
- **Gap 6, a turn trace.** Every call and every refusal is already stored
  (`dm_turns.conversation_json`); the Context panel shows only token counts
  (`context-trace` route). A test table's author holds every seat and every
  secret there, so a trace of calls and refusals can be shown on test tables
  without the spoiler concerns that keep it from a live table's lead.

### 4.G Related gaps found on the way

As found. Every one is fixed on this branch; section 7 says how.

- **A typed reaction from the on-turn hero's box** is posted as the on-turn
  hero (`SessionView.tsx:820`). The card path is right; typed words for
  another hero have no speaker choice. A "speaking as" choice shown only
  while another of the person's heroes has an open reaction would cover it
  (AGENTS.md rule 6 applies).
- **A second hit replaces the first.** One record per character
  (`last-hit.ts writeLastHit`): if two enemies hit the same hero in one
  reply, only the second can be answered (Shield against the first would
  also have covered the second).
- **Companions have no reaction card.** The reactions Hand ignores them
  (`Hand.tsx:183`) and the actions route takes a companion's card on its own
  turn only; the console's `use_reaction` form is the only way.
- **Legendary and lair actions are console forms, not cards**, at a
  person-run table (`enemy-actions-view.ts:177`).
- **A dominated hero gets no Hand for the GM.** With F3 the button plays
  their basic turn; playing it card by card would extend `gmCompanionTurn`
  to dominated heroes.
- **Starting a test fight skips the "Who may start campaigns and
  workshops" switch.** A test table is a table of its own and may run the
  AI, so a workshop kept from before the switch was set is a way around it.
- **Latent: the test fight drops `deployTemplate`'s `mapError`**
  (`test-fight.ts:250`), where a GM's Deploy and the AI's prepared fight
  report it. Reachable once 4.B draws a fight on a map of the workshop's
  own, which its author may remove.

Checked and not bugs: the deploy runs as a person even at an AI-run test,
but `startPersonsRecord` writes nothing at an AI table; the stabilize tool
rolls Medicine on the server, so the report's "invented roll" was the
model's narration, not a recorded roll.

## 5. Client apps

The client repository has no reaction, Hand, composer or test-fight code of
its own: the apps compile the server's pages, and the home feed and room
publisher read `/api/campaigns`, which never returns a test table. So:

- the fixes in section 3 need no client change and no gate;
- every new route or field from section 4 (set-up, dice script, events,
  trace) needs a `SERVER_FEATURES` flag and `hostOffers` on its control, not a
  `NATIVE_MIN_SERVER` raise;
- each new screen still has to be seen running in the app shell (`--app`),
  at phone width and in both themes, before it is called done.

## 6. Suggested order

1. Set-up save and load with positions, HP, conditions, initiative and
   party resources, plus "Keep this map" (4.A, 4.B). This alone turns the
   test fight into a bench.
2. Agent tools (4.E), so #570's scorer and coding agents can use it.
3. The dice script on test tables (4.C).
4. Gaps 2, 3 and 6 (4.F), then gap 4 with #570's remaining card work.
5. Scripted events for live tables (4.D), after a mockup.

## 7. Built on this branch (after the audit)

Each item of section 4, as built. Flags in `SERVER_FEATURES` gate every new
control, so an older host shows none of them and the apps' host gate is
unchanged.

- **4.A The set-up** (`src/lib/workshop/fight-setup*.ts`, flag
  `fightSetups`). "Save as set-up" on a test table keeps the moment on the
  workshop's fight: the foes by roster slot (hit points, conditions, place,
  hidden), the party by seat (hit points, conditions, death saves, spent
  slots, resources and hit dice, place), the initiative order, the table's
  allies setting and its dice script. Every later test opens on it with the
  party it was saved with; another party starts fresh and is told so. A
  table's Deploy and the AI's prepared fight lay the foes' part only. The
  fight's row shows the set-up and can remove it.
- **4.B A reproducible start.** Saving keeps the generator's board as the
  fight's map in the workshop's library ("Keep this map"); the stand-ins are
  drawn from the fight's own seed; the order is collected again from the
  set-up's totals through the engine's own lock.
- **4.C The dice script** (flag `diceScripts`): a seed and forced faces aimed
  by roller, kind and name, on test tables only; "Set the dice" on the test
  banner.
- **4.D Scripted events** (flag `fightEvents`): triggers (start, round N, a
  creature bloodied or fallen) and actions (reinforcements, hurt,
  conditions, appear or hide, a line for the table) in the fight's editor,
  run through the console's own handlers at any table; never a die.
- **4.E Agent reach**: tools to list workshops and prepared fights, start
  and end a test fight, save, read and remove a set-up, read and set a dice
  script, and read the turn trace.
- **4.F Gap 2**: an AI-run test seats its author in the GM seat (steered)
  with the console; enemy calls and the monsters' Hand stay the AI's.
  **Gap 3**: allies picked beside the party (flag `testFightAllies`), seated
  as copies; a companion brought or built mid-test sits unlinked too.
  **Gap 4**: summons, utility spells, lasting areas, self buffs and Divine
  Smite reach the engine, and "Resolve as played" runs a queued card for the
  GM (flag `resolveAsPlayed`). **Gap 6**: the turn trace on test tables
  (flag `turnTrace`).
- **4.G Legendary and lair actions as cards** (flag `legendaryCards`). The
  GM's monsters' Hand offers a legendary creature's options at the end of
  another creature's turn while it has actions left this round (cost and
  what is left on each card), and the lair's printed options on initiative
  count 20 on the card of the creature that prints them. A legendary card
  is one call: the server checks what the option does before it spends the
  action and gives the action back when the engine refuses its follow-up,
  and the console's two calls (the spend, then the attack or save) give it
  back the same way. What a spend bought lasts only to that turn's end: a
  Move nobody walked no longer makes the creature's next walk a free one.
  Every SRD 5.1 legendary option has a route, grouped by
  what it does in `legendary-route-logic.ts` (an attack or save, a check
  the server rolls, a move with no opportunity attack where the line says so,
  a teleport, a cantrip or spell through the casting tools, a heal, a ward,
  negative energy, a drain, Ink Cloud as an area row, Fling, Swallow), and
  a test walks the bestiary and fails on an option with none. An option the
  reader cannot read is a spend and its line for the GM, and the card says
  so. Enemies cast attack-roll spells (`enemy_attack` `spell`), and an
  ability a legendary action buys no longer spends the creature's own
  action. The console's form also stopped spending one on the creature's
  own turn while a person plays it (`due`), and a lair line that does not
  halve its damage no longer halves it. The AI's `legendary_action` takes
  `use` and `targetCharacterId` too, and with that creature named its turn
  plays the option whole at the console's door, so an AI-run kraken Flings;
  a move, a jump or a spell it names only a target for is spent and made by
  the call `next` names. "Revert this turn" gives a card-played legendary
  action back with what it did.
- **Swallowed whole.** A Swallow action (the behir's, the remorhaz's, the
  frogs' and toads', the tarrasque's, its legendary Chomp too) is its bite at
  a creature it grapples, a purple worm's bite swallows on the failed
  Dexterity save it prints, and a kraken's only the creature it holds,
  without asking that creature the kraken's own regurgitation save
  (`swallow-logic.ts` reads each block, `swallow.ts` plays it). The creature
  inside is blinded, restrained and swallowed, has total cover from
  everything outside and reaches only its swallower, takes the block's acid
  at the start of each of its turns, and damage it deals from inside counts
  toward the swallower's Constitution save at the turn's end, a failure
  regurgitating everyone prone beside it. One that holds a single creature
  refuses a second, and a swallower that dies leaves the creature
  unrestrained, to climb out with the feet of movement its block prints,
  exiting prone. A creature fighting on the party's side is swallowed the
  same; a routine's later swing at the creature an earlier one swallowed is
  refused; damage from inside counts wherever it lands (`acting-party.ts`
  names whose call it is); every write goes through the sheet audit or the
  fight's kept rows, so "Revert this turn" takes a swallow and a
  regurgitation back; and the board's move says the hero is swallowed and
  how out.
- **4.G A dominated hero's Hand for the GM** (flag `gmDominatedTurns`). At
  a table a person runs, the GM plays a hero the other side dominates card
  by card with the hero's own Hand, aimed at the party, and ends the turn
  with End turn; its player is still refused. The Hand aims every hero at
  the other side of the fight (a turncoat or dominated hero is a target).
- **Live steered tables.** The AI plays the enemies and the allies nobody
  commands there (its turn plays them; nobody in the seat narrates), so the
  steering seat gets no monsters' Hand and no ally's Hand at any steered
  table, the invoke route refuses a monsters' Hand card from the seat, the
  actions route refuses the seat's move for a turn the AI plays, and the
  console's forms correct the fight as before.
- **"Revert this turn" takes back the fight and the board, and what this
  work lets a turn change** (`src/lib/db/turn-rows.ts`,
  `src/lib/db/turn-changes.ts`, `src/lib/dm/turn-revert.ts`). Every writer of
  the fight's and the board's rows keeps the row as the GM turn found it,
  read once per row per turn, and again as the turn left it when its work is
  done. The revert puts back hit points, standing, conditions, initiative,
  the order of turns and the round, the action, reactions and legendary
  actions spent, tokens moved or hidden, areas laid, effects started and
  items dropped; creatures the turn brought in or a spell called up go, and a
  creature it sent away (a summons dismissed or ended) comes back whole, its
  sheet and every row the delete took with it; a fight the turn began goes
  whole; a fight that has ended stays ended. A prepared event that fired in
  the turn is armed again, its chronicle line reads "Taken back:" and the
  GM's refusal note goes, so a boss the revert stands back up has to fall
  again before it fires. A player's End Turn, or the lead's skip, that
  brings on an event is one turn with the round it began: its revert puts
  the round and whose turn it is back with the event, which waits for the
  round to come round again; one that brings on nothing leaves no turn. The
  revert reaches a table's last 200 GM turns: what a turn keeps for it is
  let go once the turn is older (pruned as each turn's work is done), and
  an older turn is refused, the chapter rewind taking it back; the sheet
  audit is not pruned, as a single change's undo, the sheet history and a
  fight's summary read it whatever turn wrote it. A test table's
  forced faces go back where they stood and the seed back where the turn
  found it, so a replay rolls the same; a card resolved as played waits
  again. A later change to any of it is asked about first, as a later sheet
  change is. The event log lists these and offers the turn's revert for
  them; the test fight's turn trace marks a reverted turn, and the stand-in
  turns opened inside it, and keeps their calls listed. No new control and
  no flag: the snapshot's `turnChanges` is absent from an older host, and
  the log keeps its old rule.

- **4.G A second hit no longer replaces the first** (flag `reactionHits`).
  A character keeps every hit still open on them, oldest first, each with
  an id (`src/lib/dm/last-hit.ts`; a row stored as one record still reads).
  A reaction answers the hit its player picks: the Hand offers a card per
  hit and sends its id, `use_reaction` takes `hit` (the id GAME STATE's new
  open-hits line gives the narrator, or the attacker's id, which the
  console's "Attack answered" sends), and with none named Shield takes the
  earliest, a reaction that cuts one blow the heaviest, any other the
  latest. Shield re-checks the hit it answers and every open hit on them
  after it (SRD 5.1: "including against the triggering attack"). Any
  reaction replays the open hits from the one it changes in the order they
  landed (`src/lib/dm/hit-replay-logic.ts`): a later blow that now misses
  gives back all it did, the death-save failures of a blow on a hero at 0
  included (two on a critical hit), and an earlier blow undone turns the
  later ones back into hit point damage; a later blow that was critical only
  because the hero lay unconscious at 0 within 5 feet deals its ordinary
  damage then, read off the same dice and kept on the hit, while a natural
  20 stays critical. A hero at 0 when a blow lands
  cannot react to it; a Shield they took earlier still counts. The hits of
  one GM turn still leave each other open; a later turn's attack, on
  anyone, closes them.
- **4.G The typed-reaction speaker** (flag `typedReactions`). "Speaking as"
  in Do, shown only while a hit is open and a hero of the person's (the one
  whose turn it is, another of theirs, or a companion they command) can
  answer it with an unspent reaction: the words go out as that hero's
  reaction (the actions route's `reaction`, held to an open hit and an
  unspent reaction), marked as a reaction the way a card is, so they are no
  word on that hero's own turn; "Edit in chat" on another hero's reaction
  card picks them.
- **4.G Companion reaction cards** (flag `companionReactions`). A
  companion's reaction cards go to its commander, or to the GM seat for one
  nobody commands at a table a person runs, and the actions route takes them
  off its turn; at a table the AI runs, GAME STATE marks such a companion as
  the AI's to react for. Its opportunity attacks stay the server's own on the
  battle map, as a hero's do.
- **The summon creature picker** (flag `summonPicker`). A summoning card
  whose spell calls more than one creature from its slot (Conjure Animals,
  Conjure Woodland Beings, Giant Insect, Animate Objects, a workshop copy
  running as one) asks which, from the server's own catalogue (GET
  `/api/campaigns/[id]/summons`, the stat blocks the cast spawns), listed
  by challenge rating, and how many where the spell lets its caster choose
  (Conjure Animals' one, two, four or eight). It rides as cast_buff's
  `variant` and `count`: the engine casts it whole at an AI table (the model
